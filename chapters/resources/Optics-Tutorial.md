## Browser-Based Optics Tools and Simulators

### 1. Open-Ended 2D Ray Tracing Sandboxes

* **[Ray Optics Simulation (PhyDemo)](https://phydemo.app/ray-optics/)**
  Open-source, highly feature-rich 2D sandbox. Supports custom curved/spherical lenses and mirrors, GRIN (gradient-index) media, diffraction gratings, dispersion, detectors, and ideal lenses.
* **[8gwifi Ray Optics Simulator](https://8gwifi.org/physics/ray-optics-simulator.jsp)**
  Lightweight 2D Snell's Law and ray-tracing engine featuring mirrors, prisms, glass slabs, diffraction gratings, and preset setups (telescopes, microscopes).
* **[SciFunLab Optics Lab](https://scifunlab.com/simulations/science/physics/optics-lab)**
  HTML5 geometric optics sandbox for testing lenses, prisms, and mirrors with real-time parameter tweaking.

### 2. Educational & Classroom Physics Simulations

* **[PhET Interactive Simulations](https://phet.colorado.edu)** (*Geometric Optics*, *Bending Light*)
  The gold standard for introductory physics education. Focuses on intuitive visual learning: real vs. virtual image formation, focal points, Snell’s Law, and total internal reflection.
* **[Paul Falstad’s Math and Physics Applets](https://www.falstad.com/mathphysics.html)**
  HTML5 ports of classic Java applets. Excellent for visual modeling of wave optics, interference, diffraction patterns, and refraction through wave fronts.
* **[Physlet Physics / ComPADRE (Optics Bench)](https://www.compadre.org/physlets/)**
  Web-based interactive illustrations and exercises covering geometrical optics, mirrors, thin lenses, optical instruments, and physical optics.
* **[GeoGebra Optics Modules](https://www.geogebra.org)**
  Community-created dynamic geometry applets demonstrating matrix optics, principal planes, thick lens geometry, and prism dispersion.

### 3. Professional & 3D Web-Based Optical Benches

* **[3DOptix](https://www.3doptix.com/)**
  Full 3D web CAD environment for optical design. Supports sequential/non-sequential ray tracing, mechanical mounts, stock commercial catalog optics, and Zemax file imports.
* **[OpticsBench](https://opticsbench.com/)**
  GPU-accelerated 2D and 3D non-sequential ray tracing. Supports aspheric elements, material dispersion models (Sellmeier), beam splitters, and detector screens.
* **[BeamBench](https://visuphy.org/BeamBench)**
  WebGL sandbox specialized in Gaussian beam propagation, ABCD system matrix calculations, beam waist evolution, and polarization transformations (Jones calculus).

### 4. Specialized Wave, Thin-Film, & Photonic Tools

* **[AIM Photonics Virtual Lab Simulation Library](https://www.aimphotonics.com/simulation-library)**
  Browser-based tools focused on integrated photonics: wave-guide modes, ring resonators, directional couplers, and Mach-Zehnder interferometers.
* **[RefractiveIndex.INFO](https://refractiveindex.info)**
  Interactive optical constant database with built-in browser calculators for Fresnel reflection/transmission, dispersion, and thin-film layer stacks.
